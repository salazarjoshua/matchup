Design system

Everything the extension UI is built from. One typeface, four accents, one radius scale, one icon set. If something isn’t here, it doesn’t go in the build without a decision.

01

Colour

Nine values total. Accents are state signals, never decoration: each one belongs to exactly one control. Neutrals carry the whole layout.

Derived, not new colours Idle rail tile #1C1C1C and its hover #2A2A2A are lifts of the rail. Borders are rgba(0,0,0,.08) on light, rgba(255,255,255,.12) on dark. The error banner is red at 8% over white. Don’t add tints beyond these.
02

Type

Inter for everything in the interface, loaded as the only webfont. The platform monospace stack (ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace) is used only for values the user reads as data — coordinates, opacity, page counts, filenames — so they stay column-aligned as they change. No second font ships.

03

Space, radius, size

Spacing — 4 / 8 / 12 12 is the default gap and padding everywhere. 8 for related pairs, 4 for icon-tight. Nothing else.
Radius — 6 / 10 / 12 / 16 16 panel and rail, 12 tiles fields and controls, 10 icon buttons, 6 badges.
Fixed sizes Panel 320 wide · rail 64 · rail tile 40 · control height 32 · icon button 28 · thumbnail 64 tall · title bar 40.
04

Iconography

Phosphor, Bold weight, 2.2px stroke, round caps and joins. 20px in the rail, 16px in the title bar, 13px inside badges and hover actions. Active rail icons flip to solid on the accent fill; inactive icons are white at 50%.

05

Components

Eight parts make the whole extension. Live states for all of them are in the options file, section 10a.

Rail64 wide, #0A0A0A, radius 16. Grip (3×2 dots), logo, divider, then three 40px toggles in fixed order: visibility, lock, difference.
Rail toggle40×40, radius 12. Off #1C1C1C with 50% white icon; hover #2A2A2A; on = accent fill with #111 icon and a 2px white ring at 28%.
Title bar40 tall. Logo, wordmark, then Settings and Info as 28px icon buttons pushed right. Hover fills the button #F0EFEC.
Layer grid3 columns, 6 per page, 64px tiles. Selected gets a 2px blue ring; hover shows a 42% black scrim with rename and delete in white circles; locked shows an orange badge bottom-right.
PagerAppears above 6 layers only. “1 / 2” left, two 28px arrow buttons right; disabled arrows go #C9C9C7 on #FAFAF9.
Anchor pad112px square, nine snap points. Selected point becomes a 16px blue square. With an anchor set, X and Y are read-only.
Field32 tall, radius 12. Read-only #F5F5F5 with no border; editable white with a hairline border; focused white with a 1.5px blue border and caret.
Opacity bar32 tall filled track, blue fill on #E6E6E5, 4px white indicator that thickens to 6px with a blue halo on hover. Value sits right in mono, 40px wide.
06

Rules for the build

Disabled logicOverlay hidden disables everything below it. Lock disables the anchor pad, X, Y and scale but leaves opacity and difference live. Disabled controls drop to the #F5F5F5 surface with #C9C9C7 text — never lowered opacity on the whole block.
Shortcuts⌥V visibility, ⌥L lock, ⌥D difference, ⌥[ and ⌥] page. Every rail tooltip shows its shortcut in mono after the label.
Motion120ms ease-out on hover and fill changes, 160ms on panel open and close. Drag-reorder uses a 2° tilt and a 1.03 scale. Nothing else animates.
ElevationOne shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.06) on the panel. A second, heavier shadow exists only on a lifted drag tile. No shadows on tiles, fields or buttons.
Focus and keyboardFocus ring is the same blue at 1.5px, following the element radius. Rail order is grip, then the three toggles; panel order is title bar, layers, anchor, fields, opacity.
CopySentence case, no full stops in labels. Filenames stay verbatim and truncate with an ellipsis. Errors name the file and the fix: “brief.pdf isn’t supported. Use PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG.”
IsolationThe panel injects into arbitrary pages, so it needs a shadow root, an explicit font stack on the host, and a z-index ceiling. Nothing inherits from the host page.
AssetsPNG, JPG, WebP and SVG. Eight layers maximum, one active at a time. Thumbnails render at 2× for retina; originals stay untouched for the overlay.
