Matchup — overlay extension panel

Read of the current panel: six stacked card groups compete for the same visual weight, so nothing reads as primary. The three source buttons are equal-size and equally quiet. Layers are large thumbnails but the state controls (show / lock / blend) sit detached below them. The nudge pad and the X / Y / scale fields do the same job in two places, and the ad block occupies roughly a fifth of the panel at full contrast.

Direction from the moodboard: neo-grotesk display type, black and grey grounds, saturated signage primaries used as flat fills rather than tints, rounded-square tiles, numbered badges, monospace for numerics. Turns 1–4 were drawn before the brand landed and still carry a placeholder name; turns 5–6 are Matchup, with 🥊 standing in for the real mark.
